Commissioned by the Oxford Hills physics department, this laboratory slingshot rig was engineered to demonstrate energy transfer, projectile motion, and Hooke's Law during classroom demonstrations.

Constructed from extruded aluminum stock and custom-machined steel brackets, the device features a hand-cranked winch tensioning system that allows students to pull back the elastic band to repeatable force levels measured on an integrated scale slider.

A quick-release flip-latch trigger mechanism ensures consistent projectile launches with zero human interference, providing clean data for velocity and trajectory experiments.
