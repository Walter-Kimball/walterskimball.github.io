This project involved the complete redesign and structural overhaul of the custom off-road transmission for NYU's Baja SAE vehicle. The primary objective was optimizing gear ratios and housing geometry to minimize overall weight while enduring high-impact torque spikes under severe off-road conditions.

Using KISSsoft for detailed gear tooth calculations and contact stress optimization, the gear train was calculated for maximum surface durability and bending fatigue resistance. Finite Element Analysis (FEA) in SolidWorks was conducted on the CNC-machined aluminum casing to identify high-stress regions and validate wall thickness reductions.

The final assembly integrated directly with an electronic Continuously Variable Transmission (eCVT), resulting in improved power delivery, reduced rotational inertia, and higher overall vehicle acceleration reliability across competition events.
