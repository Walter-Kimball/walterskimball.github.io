Developed over a two-year period, this automated aquaponics system combines aquatic life and hydroponic plant cultivation into a single closed-loop ecosystem. The system regulates fluid circulation, water temperature, and nutrient delivery autonomously.

A Raspberry Pi serves as the central control unit, processing inputs from submerged sensor probes to monitor pH, dissolved oxygen, and water levels in real time. Custom Python scripts trigger dosing pumps and aeration units whenever parameters deviate from set limits.

The structural layout and fluid piping loops were designed in AutoCAD to optimize gravity-fed drainage, prevent siphon airlocks, and ensure consistent flow rates across all growing beds.
