The Automatic Fish Feeder was built to deliver scheduled, moisture-protected feed cycles for indoor aquaculture tanks without human intervention.

The mechanism relies on a rotating disk dispenser powered by a stepper motor and controlled by an Arduino microcontroller board. A key design hurdle was preventing humidity from clumping the feed; this was solved by isolating the food hopper behind a spring-loaded shutter door that only opens during active rotation cycles.

Power management was handled by repurposing an old desktop ATX computer power supply to provide clean 12V and 5V DC rails for both high-current motor drivers and delicate logic circuits.
