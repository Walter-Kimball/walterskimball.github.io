Designed and built at Oxford Hills Technical School, this automatic fish feeder was engineered to reliably dispense measured portions of dry fish food on a timed cycle while protecting the food reservoir from ambient humidity and water vapor.

The unit features a custom rotating disk dispensing mechanism driven by a precision motor controlled via Arduino logic. An adapted desktop ATX power supply provides stable multi-voltage rails to power both the low-voltage microcontroller logic and higher-draw motor actuators.

To ensure long-term operational reliability, mechanical components and housing panels were modeled in AutoCAD with tight tolerances to isolate the dry food chamber from moisture ingress, preventing clogging and food spoilage.
