During my engineering internship at Howell Laboratories, I was tasked with full CAD modeling, drafting release, and bill-of-materials (BOM) management for the Model 7600 Sea Water Chlorinator used aboard U.S. Navy vessels to prevent biofouling in onboard fluid cooling loops.

Working within Autodesk Inventor and Autodesk Vault, I modeled complex fluid manifold assemblies, structural support frames, and custom enclosures adhering strictly to military specifications and ASME drawing standards. Applying Geometric Dimensioning and Tolerancing (GD&T) ensured all components met strict manufacturing tolerance stacks for interchangeability.

Additionally, I integrated the completed assembly structure directly into the Epicor ERP system, linking physical models to production procurement schedules and shop floor manufacturing routes.
