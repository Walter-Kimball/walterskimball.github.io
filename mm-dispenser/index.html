Designed and built for the 2022 OHTS Tech Challenge, this automated multi-channel candy dispenser earned a first-place gold medal for mechanical precision, wiring elegance, and detailed engineering documentation.

The mechanism uses an Arduino-controlled servo indexing wheel and custom agitators designed to prevent mechanical jamming during operation. Users select desired portions via an interactive control panel, triggering calibrated motor step angles for precise dispensing.

The project included complete ASME-compliant engineering drawing sets generated in AutoCAD, covering orthographic views, section cuts, and explicit GD&T callouts for all custom-machined subcomponents.
