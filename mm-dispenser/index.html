Each year, Oxford Hills Technical School holds the Tech Challenge competition, allowing students across technical programs to demonstrate their engineering and fabrication skills[cite: 5]. The 2022 Pre-Engineering prompt required creating an automated M&M candy dispenser accompanied by a user manual and full technical drawing package[cite: 5].

I selected an automated electronic design controlled by an Arduino Uno and servo actuators[cite: 5]. The unit features a clear reservoir feeding into four channels, which direct individual candies into a servo-actuated slider[cite: 5]. The slider moves the M&Ms past an internal barrier block, funneling four pieces per cycle into the output tray[cite: 5].

To eliminate mechanical jams, I implemented a pin-and-slot linkage driven by the servo arm[cite: 5]. Programmatically, the system incorporates a brief agitation delay paired with a visual indicator LED, giving the candies time to align before actuation[cite: 5]. Additionally, overload protection logic cuts servo torque if a jammed candy is detected, preventing component damage[cite: 5].

The device consisted of 27 individual components, with 18 fully dimensioned engineering drawings generated in AutoCAD[cite: 5]. My entry earned 1st place (Gold Medal) from the evaluation panel, and I was subsequently invited to serve as an official judge for the 2023 Tech Challenge[cite: 5].
