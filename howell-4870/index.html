This project focused on modernizing legacy engineering documentation for the Howell Labs Model 4870 Air Control Panel, a critical pneumatic regulation unit used in naval shipboard air distribution systems.

I reverse-engineered physical subcomponents and legacy paper drawings into parametric 3D CAD models in Autodesk Inventor. This included modeling internal valve bodies, pressure regulators, structural mounting plates, and high-pressure tubing routing.

The updated drawing package was checked into Autodesk Vault, complete with modern GD&T callouts, fully exploded manufacturing assembly views, and itemized sub-assembly lists for immediate production release.
